About this project

This lovely home sits on top of a mountain with no shortage of breathtaking views all around the property. It is filled with Rustic and Eclectic stone, tile, and wood. Due to the uniqueness of this home, we brought in stunning lighting from West Elm to help it's character stand out even more. Adding more textures, plants, and leather mixed amidst different shades of pink, green and blue made this mountain top gem become a treasured haven for this very special family.
