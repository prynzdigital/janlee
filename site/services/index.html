FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a project with Interiors by JanLee begin?

Every project starts with a design consultation. We listen to your goals, review your space, style and budget, and then develop a personalized design plan to guide every decision.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Absolutely. Restoring the old while embracing the new is our passion. We often repurpose and refinish existing pieces and family heirlooms, blending them with new selections so your home keeps its story.

Do you manage contractors and scheduling?

Yes. We coordinate a trusted network of contractors — painters, window covering experts, seamstresses, flooring and installation pros — and keep the project on schedule so you don’t have to manage it yourself.

Do you work within a budget?

Yes. Clients regularly tell us we delivered exactly what they wanted within their budget. We help you prioritize where to invest for the biggest impact.

Do you design commercial spaces?

We do. Alongside homes, we have designed and renovated salons and other small business spaces in Colorado Springs.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Colorado Springs, Colorado, and work with homeowners throughout the Colorado Springs area. Contact us to discuss your location and project.
