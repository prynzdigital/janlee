About Us

Our World-Class Team in Colorado Springs

Since 2011, Interiors by JanLee has partnered with homeowners to transform existing spaces through thoughtful design, remodeling and custom renovation.

Our Story

Uniquely Beautiful Living Spaces

Interiors by JanLee was established in 2011. Our company is about creating uniquely beautiful living spaces through consulting, decorating and remodeling. Whether it looks like redesigning a broken-down piece of furniture, renovating a living space, or finding that perfect new piece — restoring the old while embracing the new is my passion.

I have a love for mixing colors, styles and patterns to create something stunning. Our passion is blending classic and contemporary elements while preserving the character of your home.

Full-Service Design

From First Consultation to Final Reveal

Interiors by JanLee is a full-service design and remodeling company dedicated to creating beautiful, functional and personalized homes throughout every stage of the renovation process.

From the initial design consultation to material selections, project planning and construction coordination, we provide a collaborative, hands-on approach that makes remodeling seamless and enjoyable. Whether you’re updating a single room, remodeling a kitchen or bathroom, renovating your entire home, or refreshing your interiors with custom furnishings, lighting, flooring, cabinetry, paint colors and décor, we deliver timeless designs with exceptional attention to detail.

We specialize in interior renovations, custom finishes, furniture selection, color consultations, space optimization and complete home transformations that increase both beauty and functionality.

Our Philosophy

“Every successful project begins with listening.”

We take the time to understand your goals, develop a personalized design plan, and guide you through every decision with confidence. The result is a thoughtfully designed home that is beautiful, comfortable and built to enhance the way you live.
